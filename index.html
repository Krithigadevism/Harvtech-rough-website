About Harvtech · Practical Electric Agricultural Machinery

Built in Chennai.
Grown for India's Fields.

SMART MACHINES. STRONGER FARMING.

Harvtech Private Limited is an AgriTech company developing electric agricultural machinery — a technology-led ecosystem designed around farmers to make advanced agricultural mechanisation affordable and accessible.

Our Mission: To make advanced agricultural technology affordable and accessible to farmers through smart, efficient and sustainable machinery.
Our Vision: To transform agricultural mechanization by developing intelligent electric agricultural machines that help farmers improve productivity, reduce operating costs, and adopt more sustainable farming practices.

  • Mechanization Designed Around the Farmer: Combining mechanical engineering with accessible technology for small and medium holdings.
  • Electric Drivetrain Efficiency: High torque with fewer moving parts to maintain, removing diesel fuel from daily operating costs.
  • Swappable Battery Architecture: Swap. Keep Going. Charge Later. Keeps machines working in the field between charges.

Engineering & Technology Focus

BUILT FOR REAL FARMS. DESIGNED FOR WHAT COMES NEXT.

Harvtech is working towards a new generation of electric agricultural machinery that combines practical farm capability with a technology-led approach to agricultural mechanization.

Electric Powertrain

High torque delivery with fewer moving parts to maintain, eliminating daily diesel fuel operating costs and toxic field exhaust.

Swappable Battery

2.5 kWh modular battery packs designed for rapid field swaps. Swap packs and keep working instead of waiting on a plug point.

Farmer-Led Ergonomics

Balanced weight distribution (~95 kg class) and intuitive controls engineered for practical field use across Indian farms.

HarvOne Support

Proposed digital ecosystem for registered machines, service requests, operational manuals, and connected farm support.
