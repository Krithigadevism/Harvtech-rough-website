The Harvtech Mission

Modernising Agriculture Through Clean Mechanisation

At Harvtech Private Limited, we engineer electric farm machinery that addresses the twin crises facing modern agriculture: acute rural labor shortages and escalating diesel operating costs.

By combining high-density lithium energy storage, smart motor controllers, and rugged chassis geometry, our machines deliver continuous high torque for inter-row weeding, soil pulverization, and bed preparation—producing zero emissions, whisper-quiet operation, and unprecedented cost savings.

  • 65% Cost Reduction: Drastically slashes per-acre weeding and tilling expense compared to diesel engines.
  • Soil Health Preservation: Eliminates toxic diesel fumes and reduces machine vibration, protecting delicate crop roots.
  • Single-Operator Handling: Perfectly balanced center of gravity allows effortless operation by a single farmer.
Agricultural Solutions

HOW HARVTECH SUPPORTS FARMERS

From smallholder vegetable growers to commercial orchard cultivators, Harvtech machinery is engineered for maximum operational efficiency and long-term soil sustainability.

Precision Inter-Row Weeding

Removes root systems of stubborn weeds in narrow vegetable, cotton, and turmeric ridges without chemical herbicides, improving topsoil aeration and root respiration.

Clean Electric Powertrains

Instant torque from brushless DC motors eliminates gear slippage in heavy clay and wet soils. Swappable battery packs ensure uninterrupted whole-day field operations.

Future Autonomous Guidance

Built-in drive-by-wire capability allows seamless expansion into sensor-guided autonomous row-following, reducing farmer fatigue and delivering uniform cultivation.
